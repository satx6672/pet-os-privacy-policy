# Privacy Policy for Pet OS

**Last Updated:** September 3, 2026

At Pet OS, we take the privacy of our users and their pets seriously. This Privacy Policy explicitly details our practices regarding the collection, storage, and handling of data within the Pet OS browser extension.

## 1. User Data Collection and Transparency
Pet OS operates under a strict offline architecture. We do not collect, track, harvest, or transmit any personal user data or personally identifiable information (PII). 
* We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
* We do not track web browsing history, active tabs, or third-party website activity.

## 2. Local Data Processing and Storage
All application-related data—including pet names, breed notes, vaccine dates, and medication scheduling logs—is entered exclusively by the user. 
* **Offline Execution:** This data is written directly to and stored within the user's localized browser cache environment via Chrome's secure `chrome.storage.local` API.
* **Zero External Transfers:** No data is ever transmitted across networks or sent to external cloud databases, third-party companies, or remote servers. The data remains entirely isolated on the user’s local computer hard drive.
* **Data Control:** Users retain absolute control over their information. All saved local data rows are automatically vaporized from the device storage the exact millisecond the extension is completely uninstalled from the browser.

## 3. Single-Purpose Use of Permissions (Notifications)
Pet OS utilizes internal interface permissions solely to execute its primary utility: helping pet owners adhere to critical healthcare administration schedules.
* **Notification Justification:** The extension reads locally stored, user-input dates for pet medications and vaccines solely to calculate real-time compliance windows. This data is used strictly to trigger internal visual notification reminders, badge additions, and overdue status logs directly within the extension's user interface workspace.

## 4. Policy Updates and Contact
We reserve the right to modify this local privacy policy text at any time. Any changes will be committed directly to our extension source package updates.

If you have any questions regarding our absolute offline user data handles, please contact us directly through our developer support portal channel.
